## Brand & Style

### Personality & Narrative
This design system embodies the tactile intimacy of a grease-splattered, hand-bound family recipe journal combined with the clarity of a high-end gastronomic quarterly. It is intentionally human, organic, and grounded, positioning itself in active defiance of synthetic, hyper-rendered "AI slop" and sanitized corporate digital spaces. The aesthetic honors real kitchen mess: flour dust, simmering steam, oil splashes, hand-scrawled tips in margins, and heirloom kitchenware.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Designed for passionate home cooks, culinary heirloom keepers, and communal food lovers who crave authenticity, patience, and sensory warmth. The interface evokes comforting tactile presence, nostalgia without kitsch, trust, and culinary appetite.

### Visual Style Direction
**Tactile Editorial Warmth**:
- Rich cream and warm oat paper-like canvases instead of sterile cold whites.
- Deep roasted-coffee typography delivering optimal contrast without harsh black-on-white vibration.
- Organic borders, delicate deckled separator lines, and parchment-layered surfaces.
- Editorial narrative cadence: generous negative space, intentional typographic scale, and documentary-style culinary photography focusing on natural lighting, hand movements, and real ingredient textures.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from pantry essentials: toasted spices, wild herbs, clarified butter, stone-milled grains, and freshly pulled espresso.

### Functional Roles & Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#BD4B23` — Terracotta / Paprika Rôti)**: Used for primary calls-to-action, bookmark states, high-priority interactions, and key editorial highlights.
- **Secondary (`#4B6344` — Sauge Olive Sauvage)**: Conveys freshness, botanical ingredients, seasonality badges, dietary filters, and calm verification states.
- **Tertiary (`#E8A838` — Beurre Artisanal / Safran Doux)**: Used for grandmother tips, highlighted culinary annotations, star ratings, and community awards.
- **Neutral Primary (`#241B16` — Espresso Foncé)**: The foundation for text, iconography, and structured outlines. Softens contrast while maintaining strict WCAG AAA readability against creamy backgrounds.
- **Surface Roles**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FBF8F2` (Lait d'Avoine Pur) — Base application backdrop.
  - `surface-container`: `#F5EFEB` (Crème Vanillée Chauffée) — Card containers, elevated recipe modules, and nested lists.
  - `surface-accent`: `#ECE3DA` (Lin Naturel) — Dividers, subtle borders, and inactive chip states.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the literary, evocative curves of **Playfair Display** with the clear, balanced structure of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Playfair Display**: Reserved for recipe titles, feature storytelling, section hero titles, and grandmother tip headers. It introduces a tactile, legacy editorial voice. Avoid using it for operational labels or numbers inside data tables.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Drives interface operations, ingredient measurement lists, step-by-step instructions, and micro-copy. Its open counters and geometric humanism ensure legibility even when users are glancing at screens from a distance while cooking.
- **Line Heights & Rhythm**: Step-by-step instructions rely on generous vertical spacing (`body-lg` at 28px line height) to accommodate reading with flour on one's hands.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Density Structure
The layout philosophy favors an editorial magazine composition over rigid enterprise dashboards:
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid grid, `margin-sm` (16px), `gutter-sm` (16px). Single-column step flow and vertically stacked card displays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column grid, `margin` (24px), `gutter` (24px). Dual-column layout splitting recipe imagery/ingredients on the left and instructions on the right.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column fixed-max grid (capped at 1280px width), `margin-lg` (48px), `gutter-lg` (32px). Generous culinary storytelling margins with floating contextual annotations.

### Rhythm
The scale follows an 8pt baseline rhythm. Component internal padding leans on `space-md` for standard cards and expands to `space-xl` for curated heritage recipe features.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile physical layering rather than synthetic digital elevation.

### Tonal Stratification & Borders
- **Depth through Tone**: Elevation is achieved primarily by shifting background colors: base canvas (`#FBF8F2`) carries cards filled with `#F5EFEB`, which in turn hold nested ingredient blocks in `#FFFFFF` or `#ECE3DA`.
- **Soft Parchment Borders**: Containers use a delicate, hairline border: `1px solid rgba(36, 27, 22, 0.08)`. This mimics the deckled edge of culinary parchment and preserves clean separation without hard shadows.

### Ambient Warm Cast Shadows
Where elevation is necessary (e.g., elevated recipe modals, floating step timers, bookmark sheets), shadows are tinted with roasted espresso tones rather than neutral greys:
- **Resting Card**: `0px 2px 8px rgba(36, 27, 22, 0.04)`
- **Hover / Interactive**: `0px 8px 24px rgba(36, 27, 22, 0.08)`
- **Floating Tool / Timer Drawer**: `0px 16px 36px rgba(36, 27, 22, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The shape system leverages organic, rounded geometry (`0.5rem` / 8px default, scaling to `1rem` / 16px for cards and `1.5rem` / 24px for feature banners).

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges)**: 8px border-radius provides warmth while retaining structural integrity alongside serif typography.
- **Recipe & Ingredient Cards**: 16px (`rounded-lg`) creates an approachable, friendly silhouette reminiscent of round-cornered recipe index cards.
- **Interactive Action Badges & Pills**: Hand-picked tags, seasonal chips, and timers use full pill styling (`9999px`) to create clear contrast against structured card blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cook, Post, Save)**: Background in Paprika (`#BD4B23`), text in Vanilla (`#FBF8F2`), font `label-lg`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`, border-radius: 8px. Hover shifts slightly deeper to `#A53F1B` with an ambient glow.
- **Secondary (Seasoning, Edit, Share)**: Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #4B6344`, text `#4B6344`. Active state fills with a 10% wash of Sage Olive.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent base, text `#241B16`, with a terracotta underline indicator on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Seasonal Badge**: Oval pill with Olive Sauge tint (`#5A7253` at 12% opacity), text in `#4B6344`, adorned with a small leaf or seasonal marker.
- **Cooking Time & Difficulty**: Butter saffron tint (`#E8A838` at 15% opacity), text `#241B16`, paired with micro-typography in `label-sm`.

### Recipe Cards
- **Structure**: Surface container in `#F5EFEB`, border `1px solid rgba(36, 27, 22, 0.07)`, padding `space-md`.
- **Imagery Guidelines**: Photography must show authentic preparation—hands rolling dough, raw cuts of produce, natural kitchen window lighting. Strictly prohibit airbrushed, hyper-glossy synthetic AI representations. Image aspect ratios remain consistent at 4:3 or 1:1 with soft 12px internal rounding.

### Grand-mère Tip Callout (Notes d'Anciens)
- **Visuals**: Dedicated callout styled like an annotated post-it or margin note. Background in softened saffron cream (`#FAF3E0`), left accent border `3px solid #E8A838`, subtle vintage pushpin icon or calligraphic note prefix. Text set in italicized `body-md` in Espresso (`#241B16`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Cream background (`#FFFFFF`), border `1.5px solid rgba(36, 27, 22, 0.15)`, text `body-md` in `#241B16`. Focus state swaps border to Terracotta (`#BD4B23`) with a 3px soft spread of `rgba(189, 75, 35, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes (Ingredient Strike-through)**: Custom rounded square (4px radius). Checked state transitions to Olive Green (`#4B6344`) with an organic checkmark icon; the corresponding ingredient label receives a 40% opacity fade and strikethrough styling.
- **Step-by-Step Step Counters**: Circular terracotta badge (28px diameter) with bold vanilla numbers (`#FBF8F2`), anchoring the recipe progression cleanly.
